Traditional Philosophy & Health Science

Understanding Ayurvedic Principles

Ayurveda, traditionally termed the "Science of Life", views health as a state of balance between mind, body, and environmental factors through the lens of the Three Doshas (Vata, Pitta, Kapha).

The Three Doshas (Tridosha)

Vata Dosha

Air & Ether Elements

In traditional concepts, Vata governs bodily movement, nerve impulses, circulation, and breathing.

  • Qualities: Dry, light, cold, rough, subtle, mobile.
  • Traditional Balance: Creative, energetic, flexible.
  • Common Imbalance: Dryness, joint stiffness, restlessness, gas.

Pitta Dosha

Fire & Water Elements

In traditional concepts, Pitta governs metabolism, digestion (Agni), and body heat.

  • Qualities: Hot, sharp, light, liquid, oily.
  • Traditional Balance: Focused, strong digestion, clear intellect.
  • Common Imbalance: Acidity, skin heat/rashes, inflammation.

Kapha Dosha

Earth & Water Elements

In traditional concepts, Kapha governs physical structure, lubrication, and tissue stamina.

  • Qualities: Heavy, slow, cool, oily, smooth, dense.
  • Traditional Balance: Calm, strong physical endurance, stability.
  • Common Imbalance: Weight gain, mucus congestion, sluggish digestion.

Dinacharya: Traditional Ayurvedic Daily Habits

Maintaining a regular daily routine aligns physical habits with natural circadian rhythms to support digestion and sleep.

Early Morning

Brahma Muhurta Wakeup

Wake early, perform oral hygiene, and drink warm water.

Morning Movement

Abhyanga & Yoga

Warm oil self-massage followed by gentle stretching and breathing exercises.

Midday

Main Nourishing Meal

Eat a wholesome, freshly prepared meal when digestion is active.

Evening

Night Rest

Sip warm golden milk (turmeric), reduce screen light, and sleep comfortably.

Traditional Dosha Dietary Principles (Ahara)

Dosha Traditional Supportive Foods Foods to Moderate
Vata Warm, cooked, moist foods, Ghee, Oats, Sweet potatoes, Avocados, Nuts. Raw cold salads, dry crackers, iced drinks, excessive caffeine.
Pitta Cooling foods, Cucumber, Coconut water, Sweet fruits, Mint, Basmati rice. Excessive hot chili, sour pickles, heavy fried grease, alcohol.
Kapha Warm, light, dry foods, warming spices, Honey, Quinoa, Legumes. Heavy dairy, cold ice cream, excess oils, refined sugars.
